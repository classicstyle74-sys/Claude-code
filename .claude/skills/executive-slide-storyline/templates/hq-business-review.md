# HQ向けテンプレート（HQ Business Review / 12 slides）

Audience: Global / Regional HQ
Purpose: Explain Japan performance with quantitative evidence, connect to
global initiatives, and secure the support Japan needs.
Language: English (add Japanese notes only if the user asks)
Tone: Logical, objective, structural — describe execution gaps as
operational issues, never as individual criticism.

Every slide: conclusion-style title + Fact / Insight / Action.
Titles below are placeholders — rewrite them as conclusions from real data.

---

## 1. Executive summary

- Title e.g.: "Japan is slightly above LY, but growth relies on ATV, not traffic recovery"
- Content: 3–4 bullets max — result, structural issue, opportunity, ask
- Layout: text only, no chart. This slide must stand alone

## 2. Japan performance overview

- Title e.g.: "Revenue +1.9% vs LY, driven by high spenders and ATV"
- Fact: Revenue vs LY / Budget, key drivers
- Chart: monthly revenue trend with LY overlay

## 3. Revenue vs LY / Budget

- Title e.g.: "Budget gap is concentrated in Q2 and in department store doors"
- Fact: bridge from LY / Budget to actual
- Chart: waterfall (LY → price/mix → traffic → actual)

## 4. KPI decomposition

- Title e.g.: "TR -9.1% is the structural issue; ATV +12.1% is masking it"
- Fact: Revenue = TR × ATV; ATV = UPT × AP
- Chart: KPI tree with vs LY on each node

## 5. Store performance variance

- Title e.g.: "Top and bottom doors differ in RTW proposal capability, not location traffic"
- Fact: door-level KPI spread
- Insight: what explains the variance (execution, staffing, assortment)
- Note: frame as "store execution gap", structural and fixable

## 6. Category mix and RTW opportunity

- Title e.g.: "RTW share remains below benchmark; closing it is the largest ATV/TR lever"
- Fact: category mix vs LY / vs benchmark doors
- Action: concrete RTW initiatives in H2

## 7. Customer segment performance

- Title e.g.: "Lost customers are growing faster than new acquisition"
- Fact: New / Loyal / Lost counts and revenue vs LY
- Chart: segment waterfall

## 8. High spender and clienteling

- Title e.g.: "High spender revenue grew on fewer clients — retention is the priority"
- Fact: High / Top / Super Top counts and value vs LY
- Action: clienteling program specifics

## 9. Department store / external business opportunity

- Title e.g.: "Gaisho (department store external sales) is an untapped high-ATV channel"
- Context for HQ: explain the Japan-specific gaisho model briefly —
  department stores' external sales teams serving top clients off-site
- Fact / Action: current share, target accounts, joint plan

## 10. Risks

- Title e.g.: "Traffic softness and Lost-customer growth are the main H2 risks"
- Content: 2–3 risks max, each with likelihood, impact, mitigation
- Layout: risk table (Risk / Impact / Mitigation / Owner)

## 11. H2 action plan

- Title e.g.: "H2 focuses on three levers: Lost reactivation, RTW share, DSR execution"
- Content: max 3 workstreams, each with owner, milestone, KPI target
- Layout: roadmap or 3-column plan

## 12. Support needed from HQ

- Title e.g.: "Three asks: FW26 RTW depth, clienteling tool budget, training resource"
- Content: specific, sized asks with the business case for each
- This slide is the reason the deck exists — make the asks unambiguous

- Anticipated HQ questions and answers (Q&A prep)
